# Engineering Research

- Research before making claims that may be current, version-specific, or externally dependent.
- Prefer official docs, upstream repositories, release notes, standards bodies, vendor advisories, and primary sources.
- Open important sources and verify relevant details; do not rely solely on search snippets.
- Label facts, inferences, and recommendations separately.
- For security, infrastructure, dependency/version, pricing, and operational decisions, corroborate with two reputable sources when practical.
- Do not modify the workspace while performing research-only work.
- Report concise findings with links adjacent to claims and identify unresolved uncertainty.
